# 1.1.0

*   **1.19.3 and 1.19.4 game rules**: blockExplosionDropDecay, mobExplosionDropDecay, tntExplosionDropDecay, snowAccumulationHeight, waterSourceConversion, lavaSourceConversion, globalSoundEvents, commandModificationBlockLimit and doVinesSpread. At their defaults every block TNT blows up drops; the rest plays as 1.12.2.
*   **Smaller 1.19.x changes**, each with its own setting:
    *   spawners placed without a mob are empty until a spawn egg is used on them;
    *   renamed armor stands keep their name when placed and broken;
    *   mobs no longer spawn on chorus flowers or scaffolding;
    *   the other backports' leaves can be waterlogged, with Fluidlogged API;
    *   bonus chests can hold mangrove logs.
*   **Shared vibrations**: sculk sensors, shriekers and wardens now use Backport Core's vibrations. With Trails & Tales Backport they also hear mounting, dismounting, unequipping and amethyst resonance, as in 1.20.
*   **Fixed ancient cities in other mods' extended-height worlds** generating 59 blocks too high.
*   **Fixed cascading generation around ancient cities** while pregenerating, and a missing ancient city wall piece being logged.

Needs Backport Core 1.4.0 or newer.

# 1.0.0

First release: all of 1.19 The Wild Update on Minecraft 1.12.2.

*   **The deep dark**: sculk, sculk sensors, shriekers and catalysts deep underground, and ancient cities built from 1.19's own structure files.
*   **The warden**: blind, follows vibrations and smell, brings Darkness, and digs back down when it loses interest.
*   **Mangrove swamps**: mangrove trees on their roots, mud, packed mud, mud bricks and every mangrove wood block.
*   **Mobs**: frogs in three kinds, tadpoles, froglights, and the allay.
*   **Items**: the recovery compass, Music Disc 5, Swift Sneak, goat horns, and boats with chests.
*   **The rest of 1.19**: 1.19's smaller changes, menu music, splashes and title screen.
*   **Works with** Caves & Cliffs Backport's 1.18 worlds, OptiFine and shaders, and Quark.
